1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the independent operator of this website may collect, use and share information when you visit the website or contact the telephone number displayed on it. The website provides information about water damage and restoration-related services in the Greater Memphis area and is operated for informational and lead-generation purposes.

The website does not necessarily provide restoration services itself. A telephone inquiry may be handled by, or referred to, an independent business that offers the service you are asking about.

2. Information We Collect

The information involved will depend on how you use the website and what happens when you call. It may include:

  • Information you choose to provide during a telephone inquiry, such as your name, contact details, property location and a description of the issue.
  • Your telephone number and caller identification information, where these are made available through the telephone network or a communications provider.
  • Information you provide during a conversation about the services or assistance you are seeking.
  • Basic technical and usage information that may be generated when a website is viewed, such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, pages requested and server logs.

This website currently has no contact form. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely needed to discuss your inquiry.

3. How We Use Information

Where information is received or made available, it may be used to respond to an inquiry, help connect a caller with a relevant service provider, facilitate a requested service discussion or consultation, maintain and improve the website and the quality of inquiries, prevent misuse, protect the website and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

This website is operated for lead-generation purposes. If you call the telephone number displayed on the site, your inquiry may be connected or referred to an independent third-party business that provides water damage, mitigation, restoration or related services.

The website operator may receive compensation for generating or referring qualifying inquiries. This does not mean that the website operator performs the services, supervises the provider, or guarantees the provider's work. Any services you arrange are provided by the independent business, which is responsible for its own services, terms and customer relationship.

Calling the number to make a particular inquiry does not, by itself, mean that you have agreed to receive unrelated marketing communications. If a provider wishes to contact you for another purpose, that provider is responsible for ensuring it has an appropriate basis to do so.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone calls may be handled through telecommunications providers or call-routing systems. Depending on the systems actually used, limited call information may be available for routing, responding to inquiries, attribution or service quality purposes. The specific routing and tracking arrangements for this website have not been independently confirmed in this policy.

This policy does not state that calls are recorded. If call recording is introduced or used by the operator or a provider, the responsible party should give any notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law before recording.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Cookies are small files that websites may store on a visitor's device. The website may also use technical logs needed to deliver and secure the site. No particular analytics provider, advertising pixel or non-essential cookie use is identified or confirmed in the information currently available for this website. If analytics or other tracking technologies are added, this policy should be updated to describe them and any choices available to visitors.

7. Information Sharing

Information from an inquiry may be shared with a relevant independent service provider where that is needed to respond to your request or facilitate a referral. Information may also be handled by technical or telecommunications providers involved in hosting, maintaining or operating the website and telephone service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the website, its operator, visitors or others.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information handled in connection with the website and inquiries. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained, including handling the inquiry, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may differ between the website operator and independent providers.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights to request access to personal information, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, or request deletion or restriction of certain information. You may also have the right to object to or limit some uses of your information.

To make a privacy request, use the contact details of the independent operator or the service provider that handled your inquiry. The operator's direct privacy contact details have not been supplied for this page, so they must be added before publication if visitors are to make requests directly to the operator. A request may be subject to identity checks, legal exceptions and the rights of other people.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

This website may direct you to, or connect you with, independent service providers and external websites. Those businesses and websites have their own privacy policies and practices. The website operator does not control their information-handling practices, and you should review their privacy information before providing information directly to them.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, its services or its information-handling practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. You should review this page from time to time.
